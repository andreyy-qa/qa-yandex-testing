# Bug Reports – Yandex Mobile (iOS)

---

## BUG-01 – Incognito Tabs Persist After App Restart

**Environment:**  
iOS device

**Steps to Reproduce:**
1. Open Incognito mode.
2. Open several tabs.
3. Close application completely.
4. Re-open Incognito mode.

**Expected Result:**  
Incognito session should not be saved.

**Actual Result:**  
Previously opened Incognito tabs are restored.

**Severity:** High  
**Priority:** High

## BUG-02 – Auto-Translation Breaks Page Layout

**Environment:**  
iOS device

**Steps to Reproduce:**
1. Open foreign language page.
2. Enable auto-translation.

**Expected Result:**  
Translated content should preserve original layout.

**Actual Result:**  
Layout elements overlap after translation.

**Severity:** Medium  
**Priority:** Medium

## BUG-03 – Smart Camera Does Not Recognize Valid QR Code

iOS device
